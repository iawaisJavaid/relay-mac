RELAY FOR MAC
Universal installer · Apple silicon and Intel · macOS 14 or later

INSTALL
1. Drag Relay.app onto the Applications shortcut in this disk image.
2. Open Relay from your Applications folder.
3. Eject the Relay disk image after installing.

ADB, scrcpy, and the viewer toolbar are included. The other Mac does not need
Homebrew, Xcode, an Android SDK, or a separate Android companion app.

FIRST ANDROID CONNECTION ON A NEW MAC
Enable Developer options and USB debugging on the phone. Connect with a USB
data cable and approve that Mac's USB debugging prompt on the phone. Click
Mirror in Relay, or Connect via Wi-Fi to configure a wireless connection.
Use the same trusted Wi-Fi network on both devices.

IF MAC AUDIO IS UNAVAILABLE
Relay retries without Mac playback if it cannot open the sound output.
To restore playback, choose a working output in System Settings > Sound >
Output, then click the muted speaker in the mirror toolbar.

FIRST LAUNCH APPROVAL
This build is locally signed and is not Apple-notarized. macOS may block its
first launch on another Mac. Apple's instructions explain the manual approval:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
After attempting to open Relay, look in System Settings > Privacy & Security
for Open Anyway. A managed Mac may require its administrator's approval.

IPHONE
Relay opens Apple's iPhone Mirroring app. That feature requires compatible
Apple hardware, macOS 15+, iOS 18+, and the same Apple Account. Relay's Android
recording, capture, and quality controls apply to Android devices.

The universal binaries contain native Apple silicon and Intel versions.
